Privacy Policy

Freeze Reversi for macOS · Last updated 19 November 2026

Freeze Reversi collects no information about you. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and it does not connect to the internet.

Information the app collects

None. The app does not ask for your name, your email address, or any other personal information, and it has no way to send information anywhere.

Information stored on your Mac

So that the app behaves as you left it, it saves a small amount of information in macOS's standard preferences store, on your own computer:

  • The game in progress, so it is still there when you reopen the app
  • Your chosen variant, difficulty level, opponent and time limit
  • Display preferences, such as the board style and whether legal moves are shown

This stays on your Mac. It is not transmitted, and nobody but you can read it. Deleting the app removes it. It contains nothing about you — only the state of a board game.

Network use

The app makes no network connections of any kind. It is sandboxed without network access, which macOS enforces, so it could not transmit anything even if it tried to.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party software development kits, no analytics or crash reporting services, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing about your use of the app is shared with anyone, because nothing about your use of the app is recorded.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it stores or whether it uses the network, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
